# Privacy and Data Use

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## What we collect

Ironclad keeps the information needed to run your accounts: your name, account numbers, balances, transactions, payment memos, and records of each transfer request and approval.

## What others can see

Your balance and transaction history are visible only to you and to services you use while signed in to Ironclad. Accounts listed in the public directory show only the owner's display name, account type and currency, never the balance.

## Merchants

When you pay a merchant, Ironclad tells the merchant only whether its payment request is pending, confirmed, cancelled or expired, or that it was refused. Merchants never receive your balance, your account details, your other transactions or your one-time passcode.

## AI assistants

Ironclad's AI assistant can read your accounts and transactions only to answer your requests, and every tool it uses is governed and logged. The assistant never receives one-time passcodes. Ironclad does not use your transaction data to train AI models.

## Retention

Transaction records are kept for 7 years to meet banking record-keeping rules. Logs of AI assistant activity are kept for 1 year.

## Your choices

You can ask Customer Support for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it. Requests are answered within 30 days.
