# Account Freeze Criteria

Effective date: 2026-11-01

## Purpose

Internal only. Staff guidance for when to freeze a customer account and how to lift the freeze.

## Automatic freezes

An account is frozen automatically when a high-risk transfer pattern alert and an OTP failure alert occur within the same 24 hours, or when another bank reports the account for fraud.

## Manual freezes

A fraud analyst may freeze an account after reviewing any flag if the customer cannot be reached within 2 hours and pending transfers to new recipients total more than $1,000.

## Lifting a freeze

A freeze is lifted after the customer passes identity verification by phone with two separate checks, and after every pending transfer created during the flagged period has been confirmed with the customer or cancelled. Freezes from another bank's fraud report need sign-off from a fraud team lead.

## What the customer is told

Customers are told their account is frozen for security and how to reach support. They are not told which alert caused the freeze.
