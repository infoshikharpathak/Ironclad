# Disputes and Refunds

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## Approved transfers are final

A transfer or merchant payment you approved with your OTP is final once it is confirmed. Ironclad cannot cancel or reverse it from the app. This is why every payment needs your approval.

## Refunds from merchants

If you paid a merchant for an order that was cancelled, not delivered, or wrong, contact the merchant first. Merchants refund by sending the money back to your Ironclad account. Most merchants resolve refund requests within 5 business days.

## Opening a dispute with Ironclad

If a merchant does not resolve your problem within 10 business days, or you believe a payment was made without your approval, you can open a dispute with Ironclad Customer Support. Disputes must be opened within 60 days of the payment date.

## What we need from you

Include the transfer or payment ID shown in your transaction history, the date and amount, the merchant's order reference if there is one, and a short description of what went wrong.

## How disputes are decided

Ironclad reviews disputes within 15 business days. If we find a merchant charged you for an order it did not deliver, we recover the amount from the merchant and credit your checking account. Payments sent to another person by mistake can only be returned with that person's agreement.

## Disputes about AI agents

If an AI assistant started a transfer or payment you did not want, it still required your OTP to complete. Report it to Customer Support so we can review the agent's activity; see the AI Agents at Ironclad policy.
