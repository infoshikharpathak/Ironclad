# Account Types and Fees

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## Account types

Every Ironclad customer has two accounts: an Everyday Checking account for spending, transfers and merchant payments, and a Savings account that earns interest. Checking is the account Ironclad uses as your primary account. When a merchant asks you to pay, the money comes from your checking account.

Both accounts are held in US dollars (USD). Balances can never go below $0.00: Ironclad does not offer overdrafts, so a transfer or payment that would take an account below zero is declined.

## Opening balances for demo accounts

Ironclad demo accounts start with $10,000.00 in checking and $5,000.00 in savings. Resetting the demo accounts restores these balances and deletes the customer's own transaction history.

## Monthly fees

Ironclad charges no monthly maintenance fee on checking or savings, and there is no minimum balance requirement.

## Transfer and payment fees

Transfers between your own Ironclad accounts are free. Transfers to other Ironclad customers are free. Paying a registered merchant through an Ironclad payment request is free for the customer; merchants pay their own processing terms under their merchant agreement.

## Other fees

Ironclad does not charge fees for declined transfers, expired one-time passcodes, or cancelled payment requests. There are no foreign transaction fees because Ironclad accounts only transact in USD.
