# Customer Support

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## Hours

Customer Support is available by chat in the Ironclad app from 8:00 to 20:00 Eastern Time, Monday to Saturday. Phone support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for reporting fraud or a lost device only.

## Channels

You can reach us by in-app chat, by phone, or by secure message in the app. Ironclad never asks for your one-time passcode through any channel.

## Response times

Chat is usually answered within 5 minutes during opening hours. Secure messages are answered within one business day. Disputes and limit increases have their own timelines; see the Disputes and Refunds and Transfer Limits policies.

## What the AI assistant can and cannot do

The AI assistant in the app can answer questions about your accounts and Ironclad's policies, look up recipients, and start transfers for you to approve. It cannot unfreeze accounts, raise limits, open disputes, or approve transfers. For those, contact Customer Support.
