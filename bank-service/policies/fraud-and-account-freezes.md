# Fraud and Account Freezes

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## Signs of fraud

Common signs of fraud include messages asking for your one-time passcode, payment requests you did not expect, transfers to people you do not know, and notes or memos on incoming payments that tell you, or an assistant, to send money somewhere.

## Instructions hidden in memos

Anyone who sends you money can write the memo on that payment. Ironclad's AI assistant treats memos as information only and never follows instructions written in them. If a memo asks you to send money, ignore it and report it to Customer Support.

## When we freeze an account

Ironclad may temporarily freeze an account if we see activity that suggests the account has been taken over, such as many failed OTP attempts across several transfers in a short time, or a report of fraud from you or another bank. While an account is frozen, no transfers or payments can be started or approved. Incoming payments are still received.

## Unfreezing an account

To unfreeze your account, contact Customer Support and complete identity verification. Most freezes are lifted within one business day after verification.

## Reporting fraud

If you think someone has accessed your account, decline any pending transfers you do not recognise, and contact Customer Support immediately by phone. You are not responsible for transfers that were made without your OTP approval.
