# Transfer Limits

Effective date: 2026-10-01

## Overview

Transfer limits protect you if someone gains access to your account. Limits depend on where the money is going. Every transfer, whatever its size, must also be approved by you with a one-time passcode (OTP).

## Transfers between your own accounts

Moving money between your own checking and savings accounts is limited only by the available balance in the account you are sending from. There is no per-transfer or daily cap.

## Transfers to other people

Transfers to another person's account, including any account in the Ironclad directory, are limited to $5,000.00 per transfer and $10,000.00 in total per calendar day (midnight to midnight, UTC). A transfer that would go over either limit should be split or sent on another day.

## Merchant payments

Payments to registered merchants are not counted toward the person-to-person limits. Each merchant has its own maximum payment amount set in its merchant agreement; see the Merchant Payments policy.

## Balance requirement

A transfer can never exceed the available balance of the account it is sent from. If the balance is too low when you approve the transfer, it is declined and nothing moves.

## Raising a limit

Customers who need to send more than the daily limit can contact Customer Support. Higher limits require identity verification and are reviewed within two business days.
