# Savings Interest

Effective date: 2026-09-01

## Interest rate

Ironclad Savings earns a variable rate of 2.10% annual percentage yield (APY). Checking accounts do not earn interest.

## How interest is calculated

Interest is calculated daily on the end-of-day balance in your savings account and paid into the savings account on the first business day of each month.

## Rate changes

The savings rate is variable and may change. Ironclad gives at least 30 days' notice in the app before lowering the rate.

## Minimums

There is no minimum balance to earn interest, and no limit on how many times you can move money between savings and checking.
